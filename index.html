Commute Notifier

Native Android app, updated 21 Oct 2026

Tells you when to leave so you arrive on time. You set an "arrive by" trip, such as Office by 9:00 on weekdays, and the app checks the route in the background and notifies you.

  • Driving uses live traffic from the Google Routes API; transit uses its schedules.
  • Each trip gets an early check about an hour ahead and a final check 15 minutes before leave-by.
  • Runs entirely on the phone with WorkManager. There is no backend, and a demo mode works without an API key.
  • Kotlin
  • Jetpack Compose
  • WorkManager
  • Routes API
  • Places API

Budget Tracker

iPhone and Android app, updated 21 Oct 2026

An offline-first budget tracker with daily, weekly and monthly budgets. Money you don't spend rolls into the next period, and overspending is taken off it.

  • The rollover is recomputed from expense and budget history every time and never stored, so editing an old expense corrects every later number.
  • One shared Kotlin module holds the domain rules, SQLDelight repositories and ViewModels.
  • SwiftUI on iOS and Compose with Material 3 on Android. All data stays in local SQLite.
